Plantar fasciitis is one of the most common causes of heel pain. It can make walking, running or even standing for long stretches miserable. Physical therapy is one of the best ways to treat it.

## What plantar fasciitis is

The plantar fascia is a thick band of tissue along the bottom of your foot, from your heel to your toes. When it gets irritated, you feel it as a sharp or aching pain in your heel, often worst with your first steps in the morning or after sitting for a while.

## How we treat it

We start with a thorough evaluation to see how severe it is and what's driving it: tight calves, weak foot muscles, how you walk, your footwear or your activity level. Then we build a plan around you. It may include:

- **Stretching and strengthening** for your plantar fascia, calf, foot and ankle
- **Manual therapy** to loosen tight tissue
- **Dry needling** for stubborn trigger points
- **Taping** to support your foot while it heals
- **Ice and other modalities** to calm inflammation
- **Advice on footwear, orthotics and night splints**
- **A home exercise program** so you keep improving between visits

## Don't wait it out

Heel pain tends to get worse the longer you ignore it. The sooner you start treatment, the sooner you're back on your feet. You don't need a referral to see a physical therapist, and we accept most major insurance plans.
