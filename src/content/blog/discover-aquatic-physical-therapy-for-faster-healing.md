In the water, movements that hurt on land suddenly become possible. That's the idea behind aquatic physical therapy, and it's why we have the only therapeutic pool in Oshkosh.

## Understanding aquatic therapy

Aquatic therapy, sometimes called hydrotherapy, uses the physical properties of water to help you heal and exercise. Warmth, water pressure and buoyancy support your body during treatment, making movement more comfortable and easier.

## Conditions we treat in the water

- **Orthopedic injuries** like fractures and joint replacements. Buoyancy takes weight off healing bones and joints, so you can move more safely with less pain.
- **Neurological conditions,** including stroke and spinal cord injury. The water lets patients do exercises that would be too hard on land.
- **Arthritis.** Warm water and gentle pressure can ease pain and improve range of motion.
- **Chronic pain,** such as fibromyalgia and back pain. Water allows gentle strengthening, and the warmth helps tight muscles relax.
- **Recovery after surgery.** Immersion helps reduce swelling and lets you rebuild strength without overloading healing tissue.

## Why recovery can be faster

- **Less joint stress,** so you can start therapeutic exercise earlier
- **Gentle resistance** from the water that builds muscle
- **Better circulation** to healing tissue
- **Less pain,** thanks to the water's warmth and pressure

Many patients get function and mobility back faster than with land-based therapy alone.

## Strength training in the water

Water pushes back against every movement you make, in every direction. That constant resistance builds strength without any weights, and without the pounding of exercise on land.
